*Evaluating Language Translation Models by Playing Telephone* was presented as an **oral** at EMNLP 2025. The paper introduces a reference-free approach to training translation-evaluation models that outperforms xCOMET. [Read it here](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.524/).
